# Every floor this project has reported overstates progress by two orders of magnitude

Date 2026-09-16.  Seat 0328 of the production GP teacher, `d = 12792`.
Scripts `superelement/factor_fit/banded_audit.py`, `e1d.py`, `e1d_audit.py`, `hmat.py`.
Raw `docs/data/BANDED_AUDIT.json`, `E1D.json`, `E1D_AUDIT.json`, `HMAT_ECONOMY3.json`.

## The problem with how results have been read

Acceptance is `eps_op = max_i |mu_i - 1|`.  Almost everything measured here has instead been
reported as a divergence `D/d`, because the closed-form `D*(T)` makes it cheap.  The two were
known to differ — `D/d` averages over `d` modes, `eps_op` takes the worst — but nobody had
measured how far apart they are on the real teacher.

The banded sweep was never audited on the gate at all.  Doing so:

| radius | params | % of dense | floor `D/d` | `eps_op` | `mu_max` | `n` outside +-3% |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.00 | 338,792 | 0.41% | 3.355e-01 | 142.8 | 143.8 | 10,111 |
| 0.03 | 487,286 | 0.60% | 1.855e-01 | 137.5 | 138.5 | 10,209 |
| 0.06 | 1,002,797 | 1.23% | 7.823e-02 | 105.3 | 106.3 | 4,413 |
| 0.10 | 1,879,433 | 2.30% | 4.967e-02 | 80.5 | 81.5 | 1,661 |
| 0.15 | 2,933,999 | 3.59% | 3.739e-02 | 67.9 | 68.9 | 987 |
| 0.25 | 4,931,495 | 6.03% | 2.952e-02 | **58.2** | 59.2 | 834 |

Across the sweep the **floor improves 11.4x while the gate improves 2.5x**.  The best point's
floor of `2.95e-02` sits a mere 5.5x above the +-10% necessary line, which reads like striking
distance.  Its `eps_op` is 58.2 — **582x** above the gate.

The mode counts say why.  Modes outside +-3% fall from 10,111 to 834 and then stop; `mu_max` stays
between 59 and 144 throughout.  Widening the band fixes the bulk and leaves a few hundred modes
untouched, and those few hundred are what the gate is made of.

**Consequence.** No conclusion in this project may be drawn from a floor alone.  `PARAM_ECONOMY`,
`E1C`, `E1D` and every earlier floor comparison measured the bulk.  Where they are used at all,
they are a diagnostic for optimisation, never a distance to acceptance.

## E1-D, with the change the audit asked for, and then audited

One change only — the lifting bipartition rule — with a matched coefficient count (720k, 0.88% of
dense), identical Adam at `lr 1e-2` cosine-annealed over 1200 steps, `T` initialised at `I`, blocks
of 64.

| arm | coverage | floor `D/d` | audited `D/d` | `eps_op` | `mu` range | `n` outside +-10% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| median-plane | 82.2% | 2.794e-01 | 2.794e-01 | 103.1 | 7.0e-3 … 104 | 8,156 |
| checkerboard | 100.0% | 1.452e-01 | 1.452e-01 | 65.4 | 1.4e-2 … 66.4 | 8,432 |

The coverage fix is real and worth 1.92x on the floor, 1.58x on the gate.  Both plateau hard
inside 1200 steps.  Neither is remotely near acceptance.

The audited `D/d` reproducing the floor to five digits is worth keeping as a result in its own
right: it cross-checks `floor_objective` against an independent path — `optimal_D_coefficients`,
`dense_A`, `whitened_spectrum` — that shares no code with it.

## All three constructions on the gate axis

| construction | params | % of dense | `eps_op` | fitted? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| banded `R*` truncation + `D*` | 4,931,495 | 6.03% | 58.2 | `D` in closed form |
| `T^T D T`, checkerboard, descended | 720,000 | 0.88% | 65.4 | 1200 Adam steps |
| hierarchical low-rank SVD | 11,282,414 | 13.79% | **0.229** | not at all |

The hierarchical construction is **254x** better on the gate than anything else tried, at 2.3x the
banded parameter count and with no fitting whatsoever.  It is the only thing in this project that
has ever come within 2.3x of +-10%.

Scope, as always: three achieved constructions compared on one seat.  This does not bound any
class, and the descended arms' failure is a failure of one descent from one init at one budget —
`F_class = inf_T F(T) <= F(T)` still runs the only direction it ever ran.  See
`docs/CLAIM_SCOPE_20260916.md`.

## What follows

The hierarchical curve is the only one pointing at the gate, and its trend is steep:
`eps_op` 0.699 -> 0.229 while params went 9.75M -> 11.28M.  If that continued, +-10% would arrive
near 12.5M parameters (~15% of dense).  That is a prediction worth falsifying rather than
extrapolating, so the sweep is being rerun at tolerances from 1e-3 down to 3e-6.  A pass there
would be the first achievable pass recorded on this teacher, and it would also give the first real
answer to "how many geometry-predictable numbers does `S` need", measured on the gate instead of a
floor.
