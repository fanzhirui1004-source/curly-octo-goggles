## 3. The four findings that should not be rediscovered

**The compliance error is a load-energy-weighted harmonic mean of the pencil spectrum**,
`chat/c = sum_i w_i / mu_i`, verified to 0.68 pp over 48 cases, and `c(f) = ||M_q f||^2` gives an
assembly-free gate. `docs/THE_LEVERAGE_IS_NOT_THE_LOSS_20260919.md`.

**Almost every direction of every prediction is wrong, and box cells still deliver.** For the box
baseline, 11 535 to 16 893 of `d` directions are off by more than 3 %, `mu` spans [0.28, 4.06], and
the assembled compliance is 0.17-2.09 % because only 0.04-0.76 % of the load energy sits on
directions worse than a factor of two. That is a genuine average.
`docs/TWO_ERROR_REGIMES_20260919.md`.

**A cut cell's delivered accuracy is entirely a cancellation, and partial corrections make it
worse.** Clipping the prediction's pencil at a factor `c` and re-running the same stack gives
3.91 % (no clip), 6.64 % (311 of 10 806 modes fixed), 8.05 % (1 816), 4.95 % (5 796), 0 % (exact) -
non-monotone. This rules out rank-k mechanism heads, tail-weighted losses and inference-time
clipping in one go. `docs/CUT_CELL_ACCURACY_IS_ALL_CANCELLATION_20260919.md`.

**Capacity moves the spread, not the contract.** WIDE_BOX at 3.2x parameters lowers loss, `g`,
`factor_relative` and halfvar, but its systematic over-stiffness shrinks past zero, so the
cancellation that gave the baseline 0.2-1 % is gone and three seats leave the contract. The mean of
the two models' `M_q` beats both. `docs/CAPACITY_MOVES_THE_SPREAD_NOT_THE_CONTRACT_20260919.md`.

Two smaller ones worth knowing: seat 100186's standing 7.8 % sensitivity outlier is explained -
it is the only box seat with 9.5 % of its load energy on grossly wrong directions against
0.04-0.76 % elsewhere, so `E(mu>2)` from one `soft_diag` run is a cheap per-seat outlier predictor;
and the tau-derivative measurement is wrong unless **both** `torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_tf32`
and `torch.backends.cudnn.allow_tf32` are False, because the volume encoder is `Conv3d` and the
test amplifies any relative error by 220 at `h` = 1e-3.
`docs/TAU_DERIVATIVE_PASSES_20260919.md`.

## 4. Open questions, in the order I would take them

**a. The end-to-end demonstration, and the reason to do it first.** Everything it needs exists: the
operator, the matrix-free lattice assembly with an exact adjoint, and a validated tau derivative.
An integer-cell design domain has no cut cells, so the box-cell results carry it. Roughly 200 lines:
a tau field on the lattice's corner grid, `dc/dtau_k = -u^T (dK/dtau_k) u` with `dS_m/dtau_k` from
the network, and a projected-gradient or MMA update. Cost estimate for 4x4x4 = 64 modules: about
5 minutes an iteration, so 50 iterations in about 4 hours. This converts three verified properties
into "the method works", and nothing about the cut-cell problem blocks it.

**b. Box-cell close-out.** A second seed and a three-model ensemble to confirm the spread falls as
1/sqrt(n); the scalar calibration in the evaluation path (best single scale on the current models:
`M_q` x 0.9948 takes WIDE_BOX's worst compliance from 3.97 % to 2.89 %, though it cannot fix the
per-seat spread because the residual bias has both signs); and a decision on seat-100186-type
outliers now that `E(mu>2)` predicts them.

**c. The n-versus-capacity question for cut cells is still open, and the experiment that was run
does not answer it.** The n sweep held the total at 20 000 steps while raising n, which divides the
steps per seat, so its collapse at n = 8 (15.95 %) is confounded - n = 28 at 60 000 steps, i.e.
fewer steps per seat, reaches 2.31-5.07 %. `/root/_serial6.sh.cancelled` is the one-arm control:
the same eight seats at 60 000 steps. About an hour. Read it against NCUT_08's 15.95 %.

**d. If cut cells are pursued.** The obstruction is that the operator must be right almost
everywhere in its spectrum, so the candidates left are ones that improve the whole spectrum:
ensembling (worth one free test - average NOAUG_100032 with SOLO_100032 and re-run the stack; the
script for it is in `/root/_rank.sh` and did not finish), a much larger model, or changing what is
predicted. Note the application property that makes this optional: a part's cut planes are fixed by
its outer shape and only tau moves, so a part's boundary is a handful of fixed geometries and a
per-part specialist sits at small `n`, which is where single-seat quality (0.278 %) is reachable.

**e. Deferred and cheap.** The tau derivative in the other seven corner directions needs new teacher
runs and, under `--canonical`, would cross the frame-selection discontinuity that
`cubic_group.canonical`'s docstring warns about - all current arms are `canonical=False`, so a
forward-mode JVP through the closed-form context tangent is the clean way to extend it. 1.6b B2
(graded quadrature covariance) and stage 5 (intrinsic 48-orientation equivariance) are untouched.
