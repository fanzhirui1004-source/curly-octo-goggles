## 2. What today established

**The cut cell's operator IS exactly covariant under the cube group.** Write `q_c = C u` for the
teacher's cut coordinates as functionals of background-node displacements and pull the trace form
back: `S~ = (C (x) I3)^T S (C (x) I3)`. Under any change of cut basis `q'_c = V q_c` the blocks move
as `C' = V C`, `S'_cc = V^-T S_cc V^-1`, `S'_bc = S_bc V^-1`, so the two `V`s cancel and **the
pullback is exactly invariant to the teacher's greedy pivot choice**. Measured against the pinned
teacher on rotated geometries: seat 100000 (19 cells, q = 711) gives 9.51e-15 / 8.59e-15 / 2.74e-14
on three elements; seat 100032 (3651 cells, q = 10812, 1436 cut coordinates) gives 1.37e-14 to
2.20e-14 on five, including the S4 improper rotoreflection and the 3-fold body-diagonal rotation, all
keeping the exact image support and all 1436 cut coordinates. So the CutFEM discretisation of a cut
cell - its cut-surface quadrature, ghost penalty and stabilisation - is equivariant to machine
precision, and what was never covariant was only the basis the label was written in.
`docs/THE_CUT_OPERATOR_IS_COVARIANT_20260920.md`, `docs/data/cut_rotation_20260920/`.

**Augmenting a cut cell helps, and the evidence that said otherwise was in-sample.** No multi-seat
cut arm had ever rotated a cut cell: `cutonly_noaug` 0 of 32, `wide_cut` 0 of 73,
`ncut_02/04/08/16/59` all 0, `cutmix_fullaug` 28 of 64 and those 28 are its box seats. All ran
`--augment --augment-full-only`. Pre-registered four-arm result, held-out median over the twelve
`HELDOUT_H2.json` seats: DOSE_00 0.6400, DOSE_00B (second seed) 0.7607, DOSE_24 **0.4092**, DOSE_48
0.5109; declared `R = 0.1207` calls DOSE_24 better by 1.91 R and DOSE_48 by 1.07 R. Post-hoc sign
test (not pre-registered): 10 of 12 seats, p = 0.019, and 8 of 12, p = 0.19. The old `solo_100079`
vs `noaug_100079` pair was measured on the one seat each arm trained on; this experiment reproduces
it - presented seat 0.0153 without augmentation against 0.2064 with - so augmentation costs 13x
in-sample and buys 1.6x out-of-sample. `docs/THE_AUGMENTATION_DOSE_20260920.md`.

**Route A beats route B outright and needs no teacher runs.** The nodal pullback keeps the cut
surface loadable, exactly: a surface traction's nodal load `l_a = int_Gamma t . phi_a` satisfies
`l . u = 0` for every `u` in `ker C`, so `l` lies in `range(C^T)` and is representable. Its cost,
measured over all 184 cut seats, is 1.002-1.406x the teacher's q (median **1.31**, dense storage
median 1.71x), and the cut support is disjoint from the box nodes in every seat. Codex's retained-
background-node route lands on the **same coordinates** for 8-11 h of teacher time and a different
operator, and its only extra content is the response to nodal loads in `ker C`, which no traction can
produce. Route B is dropped.

**Both of Codex's trace-audit bugs were real and are fixed** (`234efcc`): the face-load axis was
`f_id // 2` and must be `f_id % 3`, and the load projection leaked 36.5 % of the load norm onto cut
coordinates. Corrected numbers on seat 100032 / ROT_CUT: shear_y -21.1 % becomes -27.0 %,
worst-of-four unchanged, exact-label control 0.0000 %.

**Two more code defects, found and fixed today** (`fc37463`): `--augment-full-only` was a silent
no-op without `--augment` - the flag is ANDed with `args.augment` and nothing validated the pair, so
an arm could run with it alone, present every cell in the identity frame, and still record
`augment_full_only=True`; and `model.decode` ran the trunk on empty tensors for an all-diagonal
chunk.

## 3. What died today - do not re-propose these

**The coordinate under-resolution hypothesis (strict form): refuted.** The model sees a coordinate
through explicit aggregates and through a learned-field pullback that is linear in the 32^3 grid, so
it is exactly a weight vector `w_i` per coordinate (the analytic trilinear map agrees with
`torch.grid_sample` to 4.4e-16). On seat 100000 the closest pair of cut coordinates is 0.216 apart in
`w` and 0.338 in the aggregates. Nothing is degenerate.

**"Input resolution is therefore not the binding constraint": withdrawn.** That reading quoted the one
channel that favoured it. By the aggregate channel box demands a 42-fold steeper map than cut and box
works; by the pullback channel cut demands a 9-fold steeper map than box. The relative weight of the
two channels is learned, so neither ordering may be quoted alone. The useful form of the hypothesis
is **undecided**, not refuted.

**The "85x cross-seat coverage gap": retracted, confounded.** It compared box-*only* seats carrying
5388-7004 coordinates that tile the whole cube surface against cut seats carrying 142-1834 cut
coordinates. Controlled on the same six cut seats, all three bases agree: 0.210 for the teacher's cut
rows, 0.246 for those seats' own box rows, 0.248 under the nodal pullback. The metric only measures
that two seats have a different tau and a different plane. The prediction that the pullback would
collapse cut coordinates onto a shared node vocabulary is **false as measured**.

**The decode speedup: withdrawn.** The rewritten `predict_full_q` is bit-identical to the old path at
q = 711 and q = 10812, diagonal included - but it is **not faster**: 0.974x and 1.000x. The reason
offered for the speedup was also wrong; the old `M - M.T` guard does not dominate, since 273 s is
decode. And chunk 32768 is worse on **both** axes on CPU: 0.845x and 0.913x, while changing the
answer by 6.2-6.7e-07 relative. Whatever produced the previously quoted 1.50x and 2.08x was not this
measurement and may have been a GPU run, which is **untested**.

**A wrong reading I caught before publishing:** I first wrote that `--augment-full-only`'s help text
had the 100079 arms reversed. It does not. Read from the arms' own `EVAL_100079`: augmentation off is
2.902 % and on is 12.585 %, with factor relative 0.1257 against 0.1802.

## 4. What is still open, ranked

1. **Cut cells are still broken and nothing explains why.** 0.4092 held-out against a 3 % contract, a
   factor of 14, with 3.1 % of loads inside 3 % at best. Ruled out so far as the cause: the dimension
   of the family (2 extra parameters), the number of training geometries (n = 8 and n = 28 are the
   same), tau sampling density (denser for cut than box), kappa(A*) (Spearman +0.26), teacher
   non-equivariance (section 2), teacher non-differentiability in tau (gate 2 passes), and coordinate
   degeneracy (section 3).
2. **The dose-response is not monotone: 24 beats 48.** Two readings this experiment cannot separate -
   the improper elements hurt, or a 48-fold wider input distribution gets too few visits per element
   at 60000 steps. Two arms settle it: 24 elements with a second seed, and 48 elements at twice the
   steps.
3. **Route A needs a label design.** The pullback is rank-deficient by construction (rank `3(nb+nc)`
   inside `3 n_support`; the kernel is exactly the nodal patterns invisible on the cut surface), so
   the `M_q = B^T A^(-1/2) B` machinery does not apply to it directly. Options not yet worked out:
   supervise on the pullback as a gauge-free target while keeping the current output space; or emit a
   rank-deficient PSD square root.
4. **Re-evaluate every frozen cut model under the corrected configuration** - the two bug fixes of
   `234efcc` changed the numbers and the sweep has not been re-run. This is Codex's suggestion 1 and
   it is cheap.
5. **Codex's suggestion 3**, the BB/BC/CC block-loss paired control. Ranked last.
6. **The 141 GB decision** (section 6).
7. Two literature workflows were running at the time of writing - an overlap hunt against the DFENN /
   PIML line, and an idea harvest against our six open problems. Their results are not in this
   document.

**Two standing methodological rules, both earned today.** Any future arm must reserve its evaluation
seats *before* it runs - only 2 of 184 cut seats were never presented anywhere, so there is no
retrospective held-out set. And a single seed per arm cannot separate a dose effect from seed noise:
`R` as used today is one absolute difference between two medians, not a standard error, and every
future comparison should carry a seed control at minimum.
