## The through-line

The project's grading rule had been: predict a cell's stiffness card, test all ~12,800
ways of pushing on the cell, and require **every** one within 3%. Codex's network is
off by 274x on the worst one, and everything was being steered by that number.

Today the user set the contract at the **task level** - only the assembled lattice's
compliance and optimisation sensitivities must be right - the cost target at
**100 ms/cell**, and allowed an inference-time coarse solve. So the grading rule
became something that could be *measured* instead of asserted, and it was.

## What was measured, not argued

- **Where the error is**: off-diagonal, not the pivots. Exact pivots move `eps_op`
  273.9 -> 275.2; exact off-diagonals -> 51.4. And no sub-block carries it: replacing
  99.3% of the factor still leaves 73.3. `WHERE_THE_ERROR_IS_20260917.md` §1-2
- **How accurate a per-entry predictor must be**: `eps_op = 104 * eps`, so 2.9e-4 for
  +-3%. Achieved: median 3.2-5.7. Short by ~1e4. §3-4
- **It is not a coherence problem**: re-signing the real error keeps its magnitudes and
  only drops `eps_op` 273.9 -> 103. Coherence costs 2.5x. §5
- **The failure is concentrated on the softest directions**: all 20 modes driving
  `eps_op` sit in the bottom 7% of true stiffness; the stiffest 20% already pass
  +-10%. But deciles 2-6 are still 10-42% too soft, so "only meaningless directions
  fail" is too strong. `THE_REAL_GATE` §3
- **Codex's actual modules assembled**: 41-48% sensitivity error. The relaxed gate
  does not rescue the current network. `THE_REAL_GATE` §2
- **Schaefer/Owhadi does not apply here**: maximin radius truncation is ~500x worse
  than the hierarchical partition and loses to plain magnitude truncation. The corpus
  predicted this; now it is measured. `THE_REAL_GATE` §4

## Claims withdrawn today

- "`D` is the missing gradient for the gate" - **wrong**. `eps_op` is set by `mu_max`,
  and on the stiff side `D` and the relative loss agree within 6%. `D` fixes the
  soft-side collapse, which is hygiene, not the gate. `OBJECTIVE_FIX_20260917.md` §3
- "The cost gap is 1900x" - **wrong**, that was implementation-bound. The FLOP bound is
  135 ms at peak, a 236x speedup over the teacher. `WHERE_THE_ERROR_IS` §6
- "The far field gets 0.4% of the gradient" - **over-scoped**. Codex's `bucket_loss`
  already normalises three buckets equally; the 97%/0.44% concentration is *within*
  the cross bucket. `OBJECTIVE_FIX` §1

## Literature, all citations verified by fetching the arXiv pages

- arXiv 2608.02036 - the geometry-parameterized convex-energy-element version of this
  exists and reports 0.6-1.0% on unseen geometries. Its regularization-nullspace
  principle predicts a **single-signed** bias from a nullspace mismatch, which is
  exactly Codex's 8-70x too-stiff signature - a cheap checkable hypothesis.
- arXiv 2606.21828 - confirms the mean-vs-max diagnosis independently and supplies the
  missing half of the fix: label-free fine-tuning against the discrete energy.
- arXiv 2606.25952 - a named remedy for near/far cancellation: subtract the
  geometry-independent principal part (a universal Fourier multiplier), learn the
  smooth residual.
- arXiv 2110.05351 / 2004.14455 - operator-norm-accurate sparse Cholesky from polylog
  matvecs; predicts ~2e7 nonzeros here, matching our measured 1.22e7. Empirically
  refuted for *this* operator in §4 above. `NEXT_STEPS_20260917.md` §7
