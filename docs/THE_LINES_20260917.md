# The whole map: five routes, two cross-cutting tracks, and where each one stands

Written 2026-09-17, after (a) the Fable first-principles analysis, (b) the GPT Pro
29-role decision report of 2026-09-16, and (c) seven days of experiments including
today's eight new measurements. Every number here is from a file in this repo or on
the box; nothing is recalled.

## 0. The frame, and the two things that changed today

**Goal.** A network takes one TPMS unit cell's geometry and returns its interface
operator `S`, so cells compose into lattices for topology optimisation.

**Ruler.** `eps_op = max|mu - 1|` over the whitened spectrum. It is the only
criterion that composes exactly under assembly (Loewner/Thompson sandwich).

Two things moved today, and both loosen the problem:

| | before | after | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| teacher cost | "31.85 s" | **39-95 s** per cell | `TEACHER_COST_20260917.md` |
| cost target | undefined | **100 ms/cell** (user) -> 390-950x speedup needed | user, 2026-09-17 |
| accuracy gate | 0.03, asserted | **0.15, measured on the deliverable** | `THE_REAL_GATE_20260917.md` |
| contract | per-cell certified `S` | **assembled compliance + optimisation sensitivities** | user, 2026-09-17 |

The gate deserves one caveat, which GPT Pro supplies (§11, decision 3) and I accept:
`a(g) = 1` predicted as `1 + eps sin(g/eps^2)` has relative value error `eps` and
derivative amplitude `1/eps`. My E1 measured sensitivities of **hierarchical
compressions**, whose geometry-dependence is as smooth as the truth's. **A network's
is not guaranteed to be.** So "0.15 suffices" is established for representations as
smooth as the compression, and must be re-established for any actual network.

### What is proved

- **The representation is reachable.** The Newton result is a fitted dense factor that
  passes the work gate. Capacity is not the obstacle for one cell.
- **The obstacle is per-entry accuracy, by ~4 orders**, and it is neither a coherence
  problem (2.5x) nor a loss-shape problem (on the stiff side that sets `eps_op`, the
  candidate losses agree within 6%).
- **Sampling is a hard bound**: 8,192 cross-patch blocks drawn per step from ~9.1e6
  candidates means each block gets ~4.2 gradient updates in a 30,000-step run.
- Cost: 5.67e13 FLOP/cell -> **135 ms at RTX 5090 TF32 peak**, 290-700x faster than
  the teacher, 1.35x over the 100 ms target.

### What is NOT proved

- That a *network* cannot do it. Nobody has trained one in corrected coordinates, and
  nobody has trained one on a sane sampling budget.

### Next step

A memorisation run on one seat in corrected coordinates with saturating cross-block
sampling. Cheap: Codex's harness already implements 90% of it. It resolves the
standing contradiction between `PER_ENTRY_ACCURACY` ("not excluded") and
`FIRST_PRINCIPLES` §6 ("dead"), which cite different evidence and do not actually
rebut each other.

## Route 2 - structured compression of the factor

### What was done

| experiment | result |
|---|---|
| banded truncation, radii 0.05-0.5 | damages 1857/385/294/302/381 modes; `mu_max` 59-144 at any radius. **"Bandwidth is not the lever"** |
| `T^T D T` with block-diagonal `D` | oracle floor **63-204x** above the +-10% necessary line; at a matched 7.45M budget `eps_op` 15.2, **37x worse** than hierarchical at 6.86M |
| low rank + sparse, one-sided correction | refuted by the reachability criterion (a one-sided correction can only soften) |
| **hierarchical low rank on `A`** | **+-3% at 14.34M numbers (17.5%)** - the project's first gate pass |
| **hierarchical low rank on `R*`** | **+-3% at 12.23M (14.95%)**; four seats 13.9-15.6% |
| 9/17 magnitude truncation | at 36.5% of dense, still `eps_op` **0.192** - fails the work gate |
| 9/17 maximin sparse Cholesky (Schaefer/Owhadi) | **500x worse** than the hierarchical partition, and loses to magnitude truncation. The corpus predicted this: screening theory does not apply to boundary-restricted operators |

### What is proved

- **The hierarchical partition is the right storage format**, and it is the only
  representation ever measured to pass +-3% on this operator, on four seats.
- At the operative gate of 0.15 it needs **8.58e6 numbers** = 10.5% of dense = ~14 ms
  to emit at hardware peak. **Best cost of any route.**

### What is NOT proved - and this is the whole problem

It is a **compression**, not a learning result: the ranks and coefficients are chosen
by looking at the true block. Three specific obstacles:

1. **SVD factors are not a legal regression target.** A low-rank block `U V^T` is
   invariant under `U -> UQ`, `V -> VQ^-T`; sign flips and rotations in
   near-degenerate subspaces mean two nearly identical geometries can have wildly
   different "correct" coefficients (`FIRST_PRINCIPLES` §2.4; GPT Pro §4.6 says the
   same).
2. Fixed-rank parameter counts were never measured.
3. Whether the ranks themselves vary smoothly with geometry was never measured.

### Next step

This is a **design problem before it is an experiment**: find a gauge-fixed
parameterisation (nested bases determined by geometry, or supervision on block
*actions* rather than on factors). Running training before that is wasted.

## Route 3 - change which factor you predict: `A^{1/2}` instead of Cholesky

### The case for it

| property | Cholesky | `A^{1/2}` |
|---|---|---|
| gauge | unique for a fixed order | **unique, continuous, permutation-equivariant** |
| depends on elimination order | **yes** | **no** |
| 48-fold cubic symmetry usable | broken by the ordering | **exact, via frame averaging** |
| covered by "Message-Passing GNNs Fail to Approximate **Sparse Triangular** Factorizations" (TMLR 2025) | **yes** | **no** |

Theory backs the format for our operator class: elliptic FE Schur complements admit
H-matrix approximations with logarithmically bounded far-field ranks (Bebendorf &
Hackbusch 2003; Faustmann, Melenk & Praetorius 2015; Xia et al. 2010), and neural
analogues exist (MNN-H, Fan et al. 2019; Multipole Graph Neural Operator, Li et al. 2020).

The objection that was supposed to kill it - `FIRST_PRINCIPLES` §2.2's requirement of
near/far coherence to 3e-6..3e-5 - **was measured today at 2.5x and is false.**

### What was done - today, and never before

Same seat, same tree, same admissibility, same per-block relative-Frobenius truncation
as the Cholesky result, applied to `A^{1/2}`. Self-tests:
`||A^{1/2}A^{1/2} - A||/||A||` = **1.360e-14**; exact fill `eps_op` = **7.92e-12**.

| tolerance | `A^{1/2}` numbers | % dense | `A^{1/2}` `eps_op` | Cholesky numbers | % dense | Cholesky `eps_op` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3e-2 | 8,144,216 | 9.95% | **0.2010** | 8,581,236 | 10.49% | **0.152** |
| 1e-2 | 11,032,008 | 13.48% | **0.1191** | 10,221,964 | 12.49% | **0.081** |
| 3e-3 | 14,859,451 | 18.16% | **0.0380** | 12,229,217 | 14.95% | **0.026** |
| 1e-3 | 18,703,203 | 22.86% | **0.0075** | 14,083,953 | 17.21% | **0.011** |

**`A^{1/2}` is compressible in the same format.** The penalty is ~10% more numbers at
the operative 0.15 gate (~9.5e6 vs 8.58e6) and ~45% more at +-3% (~19e6 vs 13e6).
That is a price, not a disqualification - and for the gauge and symmetry advantages it
is arguably cheap.

### What is NOT yet measured

The per-entry amplification for `A^{1/2}` (the Cholesky factor gives `eps_op = 104 eps`).
Running.

### Next step

If the amplification is comparable, route 3 **dominates route 1**: same family of
representation, fewer numbers at the operative gate, a legal and symmetric regression
target, and outside the scope of the TMLR negative result. It is then the natural
output head for the same encoder Codex already has.

## Route 4 - local physics, condensed exactly (ReCell / collar + coarse core)

### What was done

| experiment | result |
|---|---|
| collar zero-learning (true material, 0 learned parameters) | `mu in [1.000, 1.855]` - I re-derived **[0.999999, 1.854563]** today from `K_COLLAR1.npz` |
| **collar + learned coarse-core material** | `mu in [0.924, 1.150]`, **`eps_op` 0.150, 23,016 parameters** |
| **proved kinematic floor** | `mu_max >= 1.111`, 14 directions, for collar depth 1 + Q1 core |
| cost | dofs 0.27x, factorisation **0.076x**, one apply 0.105x of the teacher |
| coefficient sensitivity | 1e-3 rms perturbation of `M`: spectrum unchanged to six digits over 42 spectra |
| 9/17 amplification attempt | **void - my error.** I applied absolute noise where relative was needed; several of the 21 coefficients are structurally zero, so `mu_min` went to -131 and -396 (indefinite). The correct perturbation lives in their log-Cholesky coordinates |

### What is proved

- **23,016 numbers reach `eps_op` 0.150** where 8.2e7 predicted numbers reach 264.
  That is 1,760x better on the gate with 3,560x fewer numbers.
- The reason is physical and worth stating: **the soft modes live in the boundary
  layer**, and this construction keeps exactly that layer at full fine resolution and
  coarsens only the interior. Today's E3 confirms the premise independently - all 20
  modes driving `eps_op` sit in the bottom 7% of true stiffness.
- `mu >= 1` with zero learning is a **theorem** (the collar space is a subspace of the
  fine space carrying the same energy), which is what makes the self-test meaningful.
- **The proved floor 0.111 is now INSIDE the 0.15 gate.** The result that killed this
  route against 0.03 and 0.10 does not kill it against 0.15.

### What is NOT proved

- Whether enrichment can lower the floor (the Smetana-Patera scan, never run).
- The anisotropic amplification (a uniform positive scaling per cell is 1:1 *by
  theorem* - Loewner monotonicity plus homogeneity - so only the anisotropic part
  needs measuring, and that needs their parameterisation).
- **Whether a network can predict the 23,016 numbers from geometry. Never attempted.**
- The cost: 3-30 s per cell against a 100 ms target. **This is its biggest problem**,
  and it is only solvable by changing the contract - which is route 5.

## Route 5 - do not form `S` at all

### Where this came from

The user, 2026-09-17: *"schur 因为它是稠密算子，受内存限制无法进入拓扑优化，我们直接省掉静力凝聚的过程，不就直接跑完了？"*

GPT Pro reaches the same place independently, decision ① : **"允许隐式，支持完整
作用/能量与确定性装配"**, with the API `apply(v) / apply_transpose(v) / energy(v)`
and *"通过 apply 可取任何一列，但不默认物化全部 S"*.

### The arithmetic that supports it

| | per cell | at N = 1000 cells |
|---|---|---|
| dense `S` | 8.2e7 numbers = 1.3 GB | **1.3 TB** |
| hierarchical `S` at the 0.15 gate | 8.58e6 numbers = 69 MB | 69 GB |
| fine-scale dofs, matrix-free | 231,192 | 2.4 GB per vector |

**Condensation trades a large sparse problem for a smaller dense one, and the dense
one is the memory problem.** Compression fixes it only partially.

And the amortisation argument is weaker than it looks: a superelement pays off when
the same module is reused with fixed geometry. In topology optimisation the geometry
is exactly what changes every iteration. The project's answer to that is "predict `S`
in milliseconds so you can re-emit all `N` cells each iteration" - which is precisely
the hard problem. The alternative is to never form it.

### What this route actually is

Classical domain decomposition (FETI-DP / BDDC / balancing) uses the Schur complement
**implicitly**: each cell's `S` is applied by a local fine solve, never assembled. The
thing that makes it scale is the **coarse space**. So:

> The network's job becomes supplying a good coarse space / preconditioner, not an
> accurate operator.

The accuracy requirement changes character completely: a preconditioner that is 50%
wrong still converges, just slower. There is no `eps_op` gate at all - only an
iteration count. And the optimal-local-space theory for exactly this
(Smetana-Patera transfer eigenproblems, GenEO) is the **same machinery** route 4's
floor scan needs.

### What was done

Nothing. This route has never been explored in this project.

### What would kill it

- CutFEM conditioning: small cut cells make the fine iterative solve hard. Ghost
  penalty fixes the assembled operator's conditioning, not the solver's.
- Iteration count times `M` topology-optimisation steps may lose to `N` predicted
  operators plus one interface solve. **Nobody has done this arithmetic with real
  numbers.**

### Next step

The cost comparison, on paper, with the real measurements we now have: matrix-free
fine solve vs predicted-`S` interface solve, at `N` = 100 / 1000 / 10000 and `M` = 100
/ 500. That is an afternoon, no compute, and it decides whether routes 1-4 are
answering the right question at all.

## The decision, stated plainly

**Route 5 should be costed first**, because it decides whether the other four are
answering the right question. One afternoon, no compute.

**Then, in parallel and all cheap:**

- **Route 3** finishes its amplification measurement. If it is comparable to Cholesky's
  104, route 3 becomes the preferred output head and route 1's head is retired.
- **Route 4** runs the Smetana-Patera floor scan - no training, existing fine factors,
  and it is the same machinery route 5 needs for its coarse space. **This is the single
  highest-leverage experiment on the board** because it serves two routes at once.
- **Route 1** runs one memorisation attempt in corrected coordinates, to settle the
  internal contradiction rather than leave it.

**Route 2 waits** for a gauge-fixed parameterisation; training it before that is waste.

**And one thing is overdue on every route**: the project has never evaluated anything
out of sample. Every seat in every run is `split: train`. Freeze a holdout before the
next training run, not after.
