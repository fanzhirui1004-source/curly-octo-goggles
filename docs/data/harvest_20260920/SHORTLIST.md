# Actionable shortlist — cut-cell route, 2026-09-20

**Zero of the twelve attacked techniques survived as proposed.** Everything below is salvage: pieces extracted from the rescue notes, re-ranked against our contract. Two of the salvaged pieces are better than anything currently in the work queue; the rest are cheap diagnostics or economy wins.

## 1. The one thing to run next

**Measure the intrinsic dimension of the condensed cut family, and score the leave-one-out reconstruction in the assembled stack, not in Frobenius.** Our failure signature is memorisation, not physics: in-sample 0.0153 against held-out 0.4092-0.64 (factor 27-42), flat in n (8 = 28 = 59) and flat in capacity (31 M = 9.7 M), which is what you get when 139 samples are asked to pin an output space of 5.85e7 numbers per seat — so the only structural fix is to shrink the output space, and nobody has ever asked how many degrees of freedom the family actually has. The enabling fact is verified in our own code and is not obvious: kind-0 box coordinates are **single background nodes, nnz 1** (`superelement/equi/context.py:8`, `assemble_two.py:9`, covariance doc line 38), so `T = S_BB - S_BC S_CC^-1 S_CB` pulls back to a **common** nodal index set by pure scatter — no greedy pivot, no rank deficiency, zero-fill on absent nodes is physically exact — which makes a cross-seat POD well-defined for the first time. First experiment: extend `/home/user/curly-octo-goggles/docs/data/condense_20260920/teacher_condensation.py` from 8 seats to all 139 in-domain cut seats, sketch each pulled-back `T` onto a fixed random common set of ~1e6 index pairs (a linear sketch, so the sketched Gram's spectrum is the real spectrum for 139 vectors), read the 139x139 Gram spectrum, then for 6 seats reserved **before the run** reconstruct full `T` from the other 138 and push it through `superelement/equi/assemble_lattice.py` — the 100051-100032-100051 stack plus the tau sensitivity leg. Cost: ~90 GB of reads and tens of seconds of dense algebra per seat, I/O-bound, **2-5 h on one box, zero teacher seconds**, and it is decisive in both directions — if the leave-one-out projection error already exceeds 3 % in the delivered metric, every low-rank/basis output route dies for free; if it is 0.1 %, the output collapses from 5.85e7 index-free numbers to r coefficients and the network's job becomes r smooth functions of 10 parameters, which is the regime where the box family already passes at 0.3-3.3 %.

Two riders, free, same pass: (a) count the **connected components of Gamma per seat** from the level sets in the trace caches — this is the precondition for item 2 below and it is the number that decides whether the r = 9-18 load-space story is real or whether r is 4-8x larger and varies discontinuously with tau; (b) record the surviving-kind-0-face pattern per seat, because a cut plane can remove a whole face (`assemble_lattice.py:159`) and the leading POD modes will otherwise encode support pattern rather than physics — report the spectrum both on the union basis and restricted to the intersection of supports.

## 3. Killed, and why — do not re-propose

1. **Attachment modes / residual flexibility as a (T, R, F) triple** (Krattiger-Hussein 2018) — 95 % a rename of `T`, which is already built, already measured and already item 4 of the CONDENSE queue; its only new content (a declared r-dim **dual** load space) cannot impose a primal constraint `u_C = 0`, so it cannot represent a fixture or contact at all, and the nodal pullback keeps *every* physical traction at 1.31x the dofs and zero teacher time.
2. **Learn the extension operator / EMsFEM-ACMS-CEM numerical shape functions (`S = Phi^T K Phi`, matrix-free)** — needs the fine stiffness `K` at inference, which is the thing the network exists to replace; keeps the 139-148 s of the teacher and removes only the 40 s condensation, i.e. ≥70x over the 2 s budget, and `K` is not on disk (~0.8 GB/seat to rebuild).
3. **Data-free PIML: predict the lifting `N`, train on discrete energy** — the envelope-theorem shield does not extend to the tau **derivative** (measured order 0.97-1.01, i.e. linear in the field error: at 0.1 % field error the sensitivity error is 51x the compliance error), and `N` is 2.795e9 entries/cell = 22.4 TB if you retain `Y`.
4. **Hutchinson stochastic-Frobenius supervision of new geometries** — makes cheap the one objective measured insufficient by four orders (Frobenius 9.7 % while the median entry is 320-570 % wrong, eps_op 264 against a needed 2.9e-4 per entry); the cited 9.4x speedup assumes a stored sparse target, whereas our `S v` is an interior solve costing 10-300x the teacher's 15.9-22.0 ms per amortised column; and emitting a **triangular** factor destroys cube-group covariance, the only measured generalisation lever.
5. **Black-box randomized HBS/HODLR as a label-acquisition route** — there is no dense factorisation to remove (Schur is formed natively inside PARDISO, ~18 % of the teacher on seat 0328), ~700-1500 interior solves per geometry replaces a 203-282 s teacher, "exact" is false at a 2.9e-4 per-entry budget, and the one block where low-rank admissibility fails is the cut block that carries 60-93 % of the mass. *Survives only as an output storage format, and only if it passes the clip-style stack test first.*
6. **AgFEM discrete-extension operator as the canonical gauge** — the master count cannot be right: nonsingularity needs `3*n_master <= 3*n_cut_coords` and loadability needs `>=`, so it requires exactly 29.7 % (seat 100000) and exactly 35.7 % (100032) of cut-support nodes to lie outside Omega, at every seat under all 48 rotations; and `E` is rectangular, so the invariance identity does **not** apply and the re-gauged cell is systematically stiffer (`T' >= T`).
7. **Volume-fraction-continuous weak aggregation, `gamma = gamma(eta)`** — internally contradictory (cut-location-independent conditioning requires the hard threshold it rejects; `gamma -> inf` as `eta -> 0`, and our data contains `eta ~ 5.6e-15`), it mis-identifies the kink (ours is an active-set membership jump in the geometry stage, not a stabilisation magnitude), and it invalidates all 920 packets including the box family, our only working result.
8. **Orbit-tied 3x3x3 kernels as an equivariance fix** — the pair head is 57.3 % of parameters, is the only module that emits the 3x3 block, is fed raw Cartesian components, and is explicitly left untouched, so the composite cannot become equivariant; and the proposed module is not even equivariant on its own input (6 of 10 volume channels are vector components).
9. **Whitening the cut block by a local CutFEM factor** — the label is already `A^(-1/2)`, so the trained object's kappa is ~880 (box median 3.27e4, cut 7.75e5 under a square root), not 1e8; the ideal outcome was already simulated as the symmetric clip and is *worse* (3.91 % -> 6.64 % -> 8.05 %); "owning background element" has no referent for a greedy-pivot functional over up to 20 nodes; and the teacher is Q2, p=2, so de Prenter's own warning against Jacobi-type preconditioning applies verbatim.
10. **Sobolev / derivative-informed training on the tau-Jacobian** — the decoupling it repairs is measured *absent* (the tau derivative is a 0.44x de-amplifier of the value error: value -0.832 %, derivative +1.16 %), the estimator it proposes already ran as S4R_60K and produced the worst sensitivity numbers in the project, and its channel is 64-128 scalars per seat against a 1.2e9-number null dose that did nothing.
11. **Directional-tolerance gate (`||ghat - g|| <= zeta ||ghat||`)** — a trained net has no residual to tighten, `zeta` is not computable without the teacher (and every cheap proxy is measured to rank the physics *backwards*: eps_op 215.72 gives 25.4 % while eps_op 48.03 gives 542 %), the relaxation on offer is ~10x and was already granted by measurement in `THE_REAL_GATE`, and the asymptotic tightening demands exactness precisely where the optimiser goes — thin walls, near-mechanisms, kappa up to 7.4e8.
12. **Neighbourhood / oversampled-patch input channels (3x3x3 cell patch of tau)** — this one was proposed as a *rescue* and it is dead on arrival in our setting: the teacher condenses a **single isolated cell**, so the label is a deterministic function of that cell's own 8 tau corners and cut plane. Neighbourhood tau is not extra information about the target; the "partly void neighbourhood" of a cut cell is a redundant re-encoding of the cell's own cut plane. It can only add spurious correlations. Do not spend hours on it.

## 4. What nobody seems to do

From the overlap audit at `/home/user/curly-octo-goggles/docs/data/prior_art_20260920/OVERLAP_AUDIT.md`, after four independent refutations of our own novelty claims, the surviving gaps — the things to attack, and the things to write up:

- **Trace dimension.** Every directly-learned condensed operator in the literature lives at q <= 40 (2D) / 24 (3D) / 128 (CNEE 2D) / 386 (CNEE 3D, **scalar heat**), or is POD-compressed to 10-20 modes before learning (Parish et al.). We are at q ~ 2e4 nodal with no parameterisation. PIML-OFEM states explicitly that raising boundary control dofs "undermines the original efficiency advantage" — the line *chose* to avoid this axis. Corollary worth acting on: the literature's uniform answer to a big trace is *reduce it*, and items 2 and 3 of the table are the two forms of that move we have never tried.
- **Conditioning.** Nobody in this line reports a condition number of the learned or condensed operator except Chen & Li (analytic, no network) and us. No SPD-by-construction *and* conditioning statement exists anywhere in the PIML line; `A^(-1/2)` normalisation appears nowhere.
- **A tolerance on an operator derivative.** No paper states one. JMPS 193:105893 silently drops `dN~/drho` from its own gradient and reports no consistency check — their gradient is inconsistent with their own reduced analysis and nobody checked. White et al. train in a Sobolev norm at 4 parameters / 21-22 outputs / N=100.
- **A learned operator on an unfitted/cut cell.** Still unclaimed — but it was *proposed in writing* in EML 63:102041 §5 (naming CutFEM and the finite cell method) and the numerics were solved *without learning* by Chen & Li (CAD 2026:104038). The gap we occupy is one collaboration wide.
- **A loadable free cut surface.** Nobody condenses a cell and then keeps the condensed-away surface loadable; the literature either keeps everything or reduces to a dual/modal subspace and never loads it. This is genuinely unoccupied, and it is exactly what item 2 targets.
- **Cancellation.** Nobody measures the pencil `chat/c = sum_i w_i/mu_i` or reports that their delivered accuracy is a cancellation between over-stiff and over-soft modes. Every competing error statement is a norm or a field error. This is why importing their objectives keeps failing here.
- **Already taken, stop claiming it:** the condensed operator as the learned object (4 papers); operator-level error control (PIML-OFEM's `L_K`; EML 2023's cross-consistency); symmetry/PSD/rigid-kernel structure on the prediction (EML 63, CNEE, Parish, EML 72, Compos. Struct. 397); verification of exact algebraic structure to 1e-14 (CNEE, per geometry); cube-group equivariance for substructures (Compos. Struct. 397:120865, online 4 Sep 2026); basis-invariant comparison of operators via a subspace-distance loss (arXiv:2410.06832); graded-lattice and TPMS TO as the application (Compos. Struct. 369:119330; Cao et al. ICCES 2025).
