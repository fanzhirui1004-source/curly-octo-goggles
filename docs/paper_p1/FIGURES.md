### Figure 2

![Figure 2](figures/F01_method_overview.png)

**Figure 2. Overview of NICE.** (a) One cell: after the rigid-body part is separated, the network predicts the interior displacements from the retained displacements; adding back the rigid-body field and restoring the retained displacements gives \(\widehat\Psi_{\rm net}q\). The two-grid correction \(\mathcal W\) reduces the interior force imbalance, which gives \(\widehat u=\widehat\Psi q\), and applying \(K\) and then \(\widehat\Psi^T\) gives the nodal forces \(\widehat Sq\). \(\widehat S_{\rm net}\) is the condensed stiffness without correction, and the ordering in (a) is that of Proposition 2. (b) Assembly and design: the condensed stiffness matrices of the cells are assembled on the shared cell-face DOFs and solved, and the recovered cell displacements give the compliance and the thickness sensitivities; each design iteration updates the corner thickness parameters, with the network parameters unchanged and the coefficients of \(\mathcal W\) recomputed.

[PNG](figures/F01_method_overview.png) · [PDF](figures/F01_method_overview.pdf)

### Figure 3

![Figure 3](figures/F11_network_architecture.png)

**Figure 3. Architecture of the network for the interior displacements.** (a) Geometry branch: element and node encoders produce geometry features, which pass through two rounds of message exchange before coefficient heads compute the coefficients of the local interactions, grid transfers and convolutions. (b) Displacement branch: the retained displacements without their rigid-body part are lifted to 32 feature channels, pass through local interactions, the grid hierarchy and local interactions on stencils with weakly connected nodes (Weak), and are mapped back to displacements; a bypass reconstructs the rigid-body motion and restores the retained displacements. (c) Grid hierarchy: restriction \(\mathcal R\) and interpolation \(\mathcal I\) connect grids with 65, 33, 17 and 9 positions per axis. (d) Local interaction: the features at the 27 nodes of a stencil are gathered into four heads with geometry-dependent weights, mixed within each head and scattered back to the nodes. E and G denote element and ghost-face interactions; dashed arrows carry geometry-dependent coefficients, and solid arrows carry features or displacements.
